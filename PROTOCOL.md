# Frozen NYC Protocol

## Cohorts and labels

- Development cohort: 50,000 selected August 2026 HVFHV orders.
- Classifier development/test split: 45,000 / 5,000.
- The 45,000 development rows are stratified into 36,000 fitting rows and 9,000 BO-validation rows for each seed.
- External evaluation: 205,374 eligible July 2026 orders, processed using the frozen August OD baseline.
- Seeds: 42, 43, 44, 45, and 46.
- Predictor allowlist: the 16 columns in `config.json`.

The August labels are mapped from the frozen BMI2-DBSCAN solution. July labels are generated by the documented behavior rules and matching OD baselines. Neither source is independently verified fraud ground truth.

## Augmentation prior and GAN settings

The rounded prior is 10%, 85%, 1%, 2%, and 2% for labels 0–4. It is estimated from the August rule-qualified candidate cohort, not from all monthly orders. The BO training table targets 434,700 rows, and the final augmented fit adds the 9,000 validation rows after tuning.

CTGAN and PCTGAN are jointly trained on the 36,000 real fitting rows for 500 epochs. Both use batch size 500, `pac=10`, embedding dimension 128, generator/discriminator dimensions `(256, 256)`, Adam with learning rate `0.0002` and betas `(0.5, 0.9)`, weight decay `1e-6`, and one discriminator step. No early stopping is used. PCTGAN applies the primitive representation and deterministic reconstruction in `code/augmentation.py`; it does not use the separate generator-loss ablation.

## BO-XGB

Each BO-XGB-based arm is independently optimized using 50 calls and 20 initial points. The search uses an ExtraTrees surrogate and expected improvement with `xi=0.01`, maximizing accuracy on the real 9,000-row validation set. The search dimensions are in `code/search.py`; the selected values are in `results/selected_parameters_all_seeds.csv`.

## Evaluation

The primary manuscript-aligned results are for the August 5,000-row test set and the July 205,374-row external set. `results/metrics_mean_sd.csv` reports mean and sample standard deviation over the five seeds; the class-level detail is in `results/class_metrics_individual_seeds.csv`.

The separate 500-row July manual-review evaluation is intentionally not included in this protocol snapshot. Its current selection record is model-conditioned, so it cannot be described as a random, model-independent review sample without correcting the cohort or the manuscript description.
